# 🌿 Wellness Skyline — Global Wellness Knowledge Ecosystem

**Global Wellness Skyline** is the dedicated wellness education platform of the **Global Wellness Skyline (GWS) Ecosystem**, created to make holistic wellness understandable, accessible, and meaningful for people across cultures, professions, and generations.

---

## 🌍 About Wellness Skyline

Founded by **Dr. Neelesh Aniruddha Dharmadhikari**, Wellness Skyline promotes wellness as a lifelong, conscious, and inclusive journey.

Its educational mission integrates:

- 🌿 Wellness

- 🩺 Health
- 💪 Fitness
- 🧠 Mental Wellness
- 🤝 Social Wellness
- 🕊 Spiritual Wellness

- ♿ Accessibility
- 🌍 Responsible Digital Knowledge

5. 🎬 **[Wisdom Shorts Insights](https://wellnessskyline.com/wisdom-shorts-insight-0001/)**

8. 🔷 **[Dodecagon Framework for Global Wellness Science | Research & Innovation Hub](https://wellnessskyline.com/research-discovery-innovation-development/)** — An ongoing framework for research, discovery, innovation, and development.

Together, these interconnected initiatives support lifelong learning, holistic wellbeing, scientific exploration, responsible digital knowledge, and the continuing development of global wellness science.
